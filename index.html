About Us

Where Art Meets
Technology

SPICE VFX Studio is a visual effects (VFX) studio based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Founded in 2017, we specialize in crafting high-quality visuals for film, advertising, and digital content.

Our team of talented artists and technical directors combines artistic vision with cutting-edge technology. We use industry-standard tools — Houdini, Nuke, Maya, and After Effects — to deliver work that rivals the best studios globally, while remaining agile and responsive to every client's needs.

How We Work

A Proven
Creative Process

Five disciplined stages that transform your vision into a finished masterpiece.

01

Discovery

We dive deep into your project's goals, challenges, and creative vision. Through collaborative workshops, we identify the best VFX strategy for your needs.

02

Pre-production

Planning is everything. We create concept art, previz, shot breakdowns, and establish the technical pipeline — ensuring every detail is locked before production begins.

03

Production

Our artists execute each shot with meticulous care, following the established plan while maintaining the flexibility to adapt and refine as the work evolves.

04

Post-production

Compositing, color grading, final rendering, and rigorous quality assurance. Every frame is reviewed to ensure it meets our exacting standards.

05

Delivery

Final exports in your required formats, with full project files and ongoing technical support. We ensure smooth integration with your distribution pipeline.
